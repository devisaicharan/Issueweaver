How IssueWeaver works

Answer six short questions using voice or text. Voice is available in 10 Gnani-supported languages. You can type in all 22 scheduled Indian languages, English, or another language. Gnani Prisma transcribes audio, and Gnani Timbre speaks the questions in your selected language.

Each answer maps directly to its report field. We don’t use a language model to invent details, translate your answers or infer a root cause. Answers remain in their original script; they are not translated.

You can navigate back, edit answers, attach a screenshot, review and export Markdown. Browser reports stay on this device. Signed-in cloud reports and screenshots are stored privately in your account. Audio is processed through the server and Gnani, without being saved by IssueWeaver.

Voice is provided through the app’s server-side Gnani connection. Visitors do not need an API key. A text example demonstrates the workflow without making API calls.
